# Community Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work, not the person. Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove harmful content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome when supported by evidence and expressed professionally.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed with discretion and only shared as needed to respond safely.
